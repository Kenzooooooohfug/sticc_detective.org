Our Purpose

STICC_DETECTIVE was created with one main purpose: to make cybersecurity simple, accessible, and free for everyone.

Every day, people receive unknown files, suspicious links, and potentially harmful messages through Telegram and other digital platforms. Many users cannot easily recognize these threats, putting their devices, personal information, and online accounts at risk.

STICC_DETECTIVE aims to help solve this problem by providing an intelligent Telegram security assistant that can analyze suspicious files and links, identify potential threats, and explain security risks in a way everyone can understand.

Our goal is not just to detect threats, but also to educate users, raise cybersecurity awareness, and help people make safer decisions before trusting unknown digital content.

Our Mission

To make digital security accessible to everyone, regardless of their technical knowledge or financial situation.

Our Vision

To build a safer digital community where people can recognize online threats, protect their information, and use technology with greater confidence.

Privacy Policy

Your documents are yours. This page explains what STICC_DETECTIVE does with what you send.

What we do

When you send a file or link to the bot, STICC_DETECTIVE scans and analyzes it to produce the result you asked for: a verdict and a report.

What we do not do

STICC_DETECTIVE is designed to analyze submissions for security threats, not to provide human document-reading services. However, file content may be processed by scanning systems and external providers. Do not upload sensitive or confidential documents.

Third-party analysis

Security analysis may use third-party threat intelligence and scanning services. Their handling and retention policies may apply to submissions. Only submit content you have permission to share.

AI features

If AI features become available and you choose to use them, submitted messages may be processed by a third-party AI service. We will distinguish these features from malware scanning.

Your choice

Only send what you want checked. If a document is highly confidential, do not send it to any online scanner, including ours.

Terms of Use

STICC_DETECTIVE provides informational security assessments, not guarantees of safety. Only submit files and links you have permission to analyze. Do not rely on a clean verdict as proof that an item is harmless. Service availability and supported file sizes may change.
